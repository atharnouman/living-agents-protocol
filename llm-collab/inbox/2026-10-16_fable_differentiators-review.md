**Verdict.** Nothing LAP ships is missing elsewhere "for sure". Two candidates survive only in narrowed form: (A) a principal-issued, path-bounded agent authorization carried inside the commit and refused before the commit exists; (B) window-aware static attenuation with per-envelope budget conservation. Liveness-gated authority and the "envelope + per-call signature + receipt + idempotency on MCP" bundle do not survive: a May 2026 paper (heartbeat-bound credentials) and MCP projects with more users (Signet, EMILIA) cover them. gittuf already does signed, path-scoped write authorization for bots, verifiable from the repository.

| Candidate | Closest work | What LAP adds | Confidence missing / that it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| (A) in-commit authorization | gittuf (policy refs, post hoc), Notari (signed scope at PR time), Morrison draft (signed attribution, human key), git-ai (unsigned notes), Copilot signing its commits | the grant travels in the commit, the agent key signs tree and paths, out-of-scope paths are refused before the commit | medium-high missing as a combination; low as a moat |
| (B) budget conservation | Schrock draft (store-enforced aggregate), Chio budget_split, Izmaylov draft (verifier sums receipts), Hardt draft (budgets not delegable), AAE (no cumulative budget), A2A #2121 (fan-out unsolved) | a stateless rule for windowed cumulative caps (an hourly child of a daily parent debits 24x), exact integers, multi-scope conservation inside one envelope, fuzzed across two ports | medium missing for the window-aware static rule; low to medium that it matters |
| Liveness-gated authority | HBHC paper (May 2026), A2A #1934 | witness-confirmed suspension and resumption in a demo | low missing; low matters |
| MCP bundle | Signet, EMILIA Gate, Niyikiza draft, Farley and XAIP drafts | server-signed receipt over request and response by default; signed idempotency with a three-state claim | medium missing as a bundle; low as a differentiator |

**Claim (defensible):** "LAP's scope algebra is a small, executable attenuation checker whose windowed-budget rule and per-envelope conservation agree across two independent ports under a committed fuzz digest; lap-git demonstrates that a principal-issued, path-bounded agent authorization can travel inside a commit and be refused before the commit exists."

**Stop claiming:** "the existence layer" and "Nothing standardizes an agent's existence" (README line 19); "budgets conserve across children" (only within one envelope); liveness suspension as novel; "among the first protocol specifications hardened by cross-model adversarial review"; Bitcoin stamps as a merit; any "first" or "only".
