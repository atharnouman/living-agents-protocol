# LAP direction review (2026-10-16, day 9)

**Verdict (opinion; evidence below).** The stated stance (no new features; join converging work) is right, but the direction as practised is still "promote LAP as a standard in five venues at once", and nine days of data say that cannot pay off. Every external signal is zero except one AI-assisted review and a pending map listing. The venues chosen cannot deliver the Day-60 gate: A2A maintainers have commented on none of ten sampled extension proposals, the W3C CG list carried eight messages in all of October with its meeting cancelled, and MCP now requires a working group behind any SEP. The "existence layer" is no longer white space: 378 IETF documents with "agent" in the name since January, roughly 25 agent "receipt" drafts (delegation receipts at -14, bounded-capability receipts at -06, signed execution receipts for tool calls), an AAE envelope draft, and A2A issues on liveness, budget propagation, attenuated authority and identity credentials (the last filed 15 Oct). Strongest case against: (1) LAP markets a layer while what it has shipped and tested is two headers, a receipt and an attenuation algebra, all of which exist elsewhere in prose; the first outside reviewer found three overclaims in the first outreach text. (2) The owner's own August review (`output/LAP-critical-review.md`) predicted this: the gaps LAP uniquely owns (presence, estates, handshake) have the least demand, and the crowded gaps (identity binding, audit) are where LAP now competes with no distribution. (3) Attention is going to launch administration (20 launch-log rows and 18 commits on launch day; 45 of 60 commits between 00:00 and 04:00 local) while the one experiment that could change the picture, another party's verifier accepting LAP's bytes, has not started.

## 2. Differentiator; keep or fold?

No idea in LAP is unique: envelope/mandate (AAE; draft-nelson-agent-delegation-receipts-14; draft-schrock-ep-bounded-capability-receipts-06), per-call receipt (draft-xkumakichi-xaip-receipts-03; draft-farley-acta-signed-receipts-03, written for MCP; draft-izmaylov-agent-permission-receipts-00), liveness (A2A #1934), budgets (A2A #2121), attenuation (A2A #2079), identity credentials (A2A #2355). What is rarer (opinion): (a) an executable closed attenuation algebra with budget conservation, fuzzed across two ports with a committed decision digest; the others state attenuation in prose; (b) an on-wire MCP binding running on the official SDK 1.x/2.x in CI. Keep the repo as the home of LIP-3, LIP-4 and the binding; let LIP-1 proof classes, LIP-2 MEET, pulses, estates and tenure go dormant (mark, don't delete). Fold the receipt into ARC as a typed extension: ARC's Appendix A.2 explicitly invites "runtime-evidence layers that verify live wire bytes" to carry their digest as extension data. Since IETF AUDIT is converging on vCon as a record format (map entry `entries/ietf-audit.yaml`), keep the mapping a format-agnostic ten-field object and show it in ARC first. Offer the fuzz corpus to the delegation-receipts/AAE authors as test vectors: that is "contribute, don't compete" made concrete. Rename the pitch from "existence layer" to "attenuation algebra plus MCP authority binding".

## 3. The Day-60 gate

It is binary, lagging and will fail regardless of direction: ARC, a more mature artifact with a 56-comment SEP thread and three reported clean-room reproductions, has 0 stars three weeks in. A fail on Dec 7 measures the market, not the direction. The 45-day rule should distinguish dead venues (W3C CG), swamped ones (A2A: 273 open issues, no triage) and live ones (MCP IGs, ARC). Weekly, 15 minutes: substantive replies by named humans (with the question asked); maintainer-association comments on #2323; ARC issue state; IG agenda slot; GitHub Insights unique visitors/cloners and top referrer (free, owner-only, currently blank in Appendix F); listings/citations; external vector reproductions (add an issue template); hours spent. Drop stars and downloads. Replacement rule (opinion): by 2026-12-07, two of {ARC registration substantively reviewed; IG slot held; one maintainer-level reply anywhere; one external reproduction or issue} -> continue; otherwise dormant with a write-up. Add a checkpoint after IETF 127 (14-20 Nov, AUDIT BoF).

## 6. The ARC extension test

Worth it (opinion), bounded as above, because it is the first artifact another party's verifier would check and it was promised publicly. Fit is good: ARC §2.1 core plus an extension (flat name, e.g. `authority-envelope`) carrying principal DID (iss), agent DID (sub), passport hash, act, res, cap as strings, request and response hashes, receipt signature, server DID, idempotency key, replay flag and error code. Convincing to those people: the digests reproduced before any fit is claimed; a chain the ARC verifier passes; a negative pair (a forged receipt in an otherwise valid chain: the chain verifies, LAP's `verify_receipt` fails; tamper a record: the chain breaks), which is exactly the "authority side" argument; no proposal to touch §2.1/2.3/2.4; a stated choice for `principal_id` (agent DID, with principal DID in the extension, following `caller-governance`'s `invoked_by_principal_id` pattern) with the reason. Post to the IG thread only after the ARC maintainer has seen the issue; if it cannot be done in two weeks, say so there.
