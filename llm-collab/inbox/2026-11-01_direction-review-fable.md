2. `authority_verified` "`true` or `false`" is ambiguous: caller-governance's `flagged` is a JSON boolean, and these records carry strings. Write: "`authority_verified` (REQUIRED, string enum `\"true\"` | `\"false\"`; a string, as in `runtime-security`, not the `flagged` boolean)".
3. No per-field types or forms. Add, in `runtime-security`'s style: `authority_format` string of the form `<format>/<major>` (here `lap-microcore/0`); `decision_code` string: `ok`, `error`, or a refusal code of the named format (for `lap-microcore/0` the `LAP_ERR_*` codes of LIP-4 §2; open vocabulary, `ok` and `error` reserved); `action`, `resource` strings as the format defines them; the five hash fields `<algorithm>:<hex>` (`sha256:` plus 64 lowercase hex): `authority_hash` over the artifact bytes, `scope_hash` over the RFC 8785 form of the envelope, `request_hash` over the RFC 8785 arguments, `response_hash` over the RFC 8785 structured content (else the text), `signature_base_hash` over the signed base; `issuer_id`, `subject_id`, `audience_id`, `receipt_signer_id` opaque identifier strings (DIDs here); `amount`, `cap_max_per_tx` decimal integer strings as presented (a refused negative amount is recorded as presented); `unit` string; `receipt_signature`, `request_signature` base64url without padding; `idempotency_key` opaque string; `proof_class` string whose vocabulary belongs to the format (`self-asserted` | `domain-validated` | `org-validated` | `gov-validated` for `lap-microcore/0`).
